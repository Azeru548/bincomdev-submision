Chapter One

About Me

The story so far, in brief.

Kennedy Azeru is a fullstack software engineer and Web3 developer based in Port Harcourt, Nigeria, with a B.Tech in Cybersecurity from the Federal University of Technology, Owerri (FUTO). His practice spans Go and Node backends, Next.js and React frontends, production AI systems, and Sui Move smart contracts — always grounded in secure system design and threat-aware engineering.

"Systems that hold under real load — with clean architecture and measurable outcomes."

Outside client work, Kennedy publishes open malware investigations — full forensic records of real specimens, complete with timelines, IOCs and YARA rules — and builds free alternatives to expensive tooling, from agent workflows to a native TTS MCP server. He is open to opportunities in fullstack, AI, and on-chain infrastructure roles.
